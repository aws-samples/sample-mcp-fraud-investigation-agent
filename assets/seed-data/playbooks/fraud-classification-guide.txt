FRAUD CLASSIFICATION AND SCORING GUIDE

Classification Categories:
1. ACCOUNT TAKEOVER - Unauthorized access to existing account
2. SYNTHETIC IDENTITY - Fabricated identity using real and fake information
3. FRIENDLY FRAUD - Legitimate customer disputing valid charges
4. CARD NOT PRESENT - Stolen card details used for online purchase
5. RETURN FRAUD - Abuse of return policies

Scoring Model:
- Each anomaly signal adds points to the fraud score
- Spending anomaly: +15 points
- Geographic anomaly: +20 points
- Device anomaly: +15 points
- Time anomaly: +10 points
- Credential change anomaly: +25 points
- Prior fraud history: +25 points

Confidence Levels:
- 0-25 points: LOW - Monitor only
- 26-50 points: MEDIUM - Manual review required
- 51-75 points: HIGH - Automatic hold, expedited review
- 76+ points: CRITICAL - Immediate action required

Repeat Target Indicator:
- Customers with prior unauthorized activity cases are considered REPEAT TARGETS
- Repeat targets receive +25 bonus points to fraud score
- Repeat targets trigger automatic escalation regardless of score
