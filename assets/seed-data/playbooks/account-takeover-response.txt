ACCOUNT TAKEOVER RESPONSE PROTOCOL

Definition: Account takeover (ATO) occurs when an unauthorized party gains access to a customer account through credential theft, phishing, or session hijacking.

Detection Criteria:
- 3 or more anomaly signals from different categories = HIGH CONFIDENCE account takeover
- Anomaly categories: spending pattern, shipping address, device fingerprint, login time, credential changes, support history

Required Actions for HIGH CONFIDENCE ATO:
1. HOLD all pending transactions immediately
2. FREEZE account for further review
3. NOTIFY customer via verified contact method (phone on file, not email)
4. ESCALATE to fraud team with full investigation report

Escalation Rules:
- Prior support cases for unauthorized activity INCREASE confidence score by 20%
- Password change from new IP within 24 hours of suspicious transaction = CRITICAL indicator
- Multiple anomaly signals from same session = coordinated attack pattern

Response Time SLA:
- HIGH CONFIDENCE: Action within 5 minutes
- MEDIUM CONFIDENCE: Review within 30 minutes
- LOW CONFIDENCE: Flag for daily review
